Our Story

A Small Kitchen, Making One Thing Well

Pizza's and More opened as a two-person operation with one oven and a short menu — and that's still exactly what it is. No delivery apps, no chains, just pizza made to order for the neighbourhood.

Every dough ball is proofed overnight, every sauce is made that morning, and if we're out of something, we'll tell you instead of substituting it.
